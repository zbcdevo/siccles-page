Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

shield 1. Overview & Commitment

At Siccles (developed by Zabba Labs), we respect your personal privacy. Siccles is designed from the ground up as an offline-first sleep cycle calculator and alarm app. We do not collect, harvest, sell, or transmit any personal data.

database 2. Data Storage & Usage

All application data — including your sleep latency preferences, customized alarm times, sound choices, and wake-up settings — is stored strictly on your local device storage.

  • No user accounts or logins are required.
  • No telemetry, analytics, or behavioral tracking tools are integrated.
  • Your data never leaves your device and is erased if you uninstall the app.

key 3. App Permissions

Siccles requests standard device permissions solely for providing native alarm and sleep cycle calculation functionality:

  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used to trigger alarm ringers precisely at your calculated wake-up time.
  • Full-Screen Notifications (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT): Used to display the alarm wake-up screen over your lock screen when an alarm rings.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to display active alarm alerts and notifications.

share_off 4. Third-Party Services

Siccles does not integrate any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or external cloud databases that gather personal identifier information.
